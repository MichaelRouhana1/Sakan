## Layout and responsive sizing

Each card is an unboxed link with a square, cover-cropped image and text directly underneath. The outer link has a 14.12 px radius, no border, no shadow and no inset padding. The image has a **26 px squircle** in this browser. Amber declares a 15.06 px rounded fallback for browsers without CSS corner shapes. The middle space between cards is **12 px** at every tested width. The Bath rail starts 48 px from the left and bleeds to the viewport edge when scrolled.

## Hover and leave

The photographed image and media frame stay at their original size, position and opacity on both media and title hover. The card has no lift, image zoom, hover shadow or active photo slide. No photo arrows or dots are present, including on touch. The image’s declared **250 ms ease** transitions over opacity and transform belong to image loading (opacity **0 → 1**, scale **0.9 → 1**), and the card’s **200 ms ease** opacity declaration does not change settled hover opacity.

Entering the first card mounts the heart’s SVG layer and hides the static outline. In a two-second frame trace, the SVG appeared at 23 ms and the outline became hidden at 33 ms, measured from the start of sampling just before the pointer moved. The sampled SVG path and group attributes then stayed constant through hover and leave. The layer remains mounted after leave. Its canvas is **150%** of the button width, placed at **50% / 44.5%** with `translate(-50%, -50%)`.

Direct hover on the heart control reaches **scale(1.05)** and returns to identity on leave. Both directions use **220 ms cubic-bezier(.22, 1, .36, 1)**. The authored press rules specify **100 ms** and **scale(.95)**, or **scale(.97)** while hovered.
